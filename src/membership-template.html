Boat Detailing Membership Plans

One boat, your level of care. Every Propwash membership keeps salt off on a schedule you set, with photos after every visit so you always know it's been looked after. The difference between the tiers is how much you want us to handle — from a clean boat on repeat, to a boat that's fully managed between trips. Every plan is custom quoted to your boat, and every new plan starts with a Full Detail.

Silver

Keeps it clean.
Essential care.

Silver is the simplest way to never let salt build up again. Recurring washes on the schedule you set — weekly, bi-weekly or monthly — with a full exterior wash and salt rinse every single visit, and a photo when we're done. You're locked into your rotation, so there's no chasing us down. Set it once; we keep the salt off from there.

Gold

Keeps it maintained.
Complete maintenance. Our core plan.

Gold keeps a working boat maintained year-round without you thinking about it. Everything in Silver, plus a complete Full Detail every month — oxidation and rust off the gelcoat, mold off the seats, hatches and compartments cleaned out, a deep interior. You get priority scheduling ahead of standard washes and your own assigned detailer who learns the boat over time. You put in the hours on the water; we make sure the salt never catches up.

Platinum

Invite only
We manage your boat.
By invitation. Capped at 15 boats.

Platinum is the closest thing there is to having someone manage your boat for you. Everything in Gold, plus two Trip Ready preps a month — you show up and she's ready to run: wiped down, canvas off, coolers iced, water and drinks stocked, electronics on, lines stowed. On-call washdowns between visits, a 24-hour response window, spot reporting from your Slip, and top priority on the calendar. Invite-only and capped at 15 boats, so the list stays short on purpose.

Cadence

How wash frequency
works.

Wash cadence is a separate choice from your tier. Silver runs weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. Gold and Platinum let us build a custom cadence around how you actually store and use the boat — in a Slip, on a lift, or in the driveway. The tier sets the scope of care and your scheduling priority; the frequency follows your boat. We put both in your plan.

Good to know

What every
plan includes.

Every new plan starts with a separately quoted Full Detail so we begin from a known baseline. Machine wax is not included in any tier. Coated boats receive quarterly ceramic boosters. Month-to-month and contract options are available, and every plan is custom quoted.

Membership
questions.

What is included in a boat detailing membership?

Every Propwash membership includes recurring exterior washes and salt rinses on the schedule you set, with photos after every visit. Gold and Platinum add a complete Full Detail every month, and Platinum adds Trip Ready prep and between-visit care.

How much does a boat detailing membership cost?

Every plan is custom quoted based on the boat's length, condition and how it is used. Contact Propwash Marine Detailing at (561) 291-8554 for a free quote.

What is the difference between Silver, Gold and Platinum?

Silver keeps the boat clean with recurring washes. Gold adds a monthly Full Detail and priority scheduling. Platinum is invite-only and adds Trip Ready prep, between-visit washdowns and top scheduling priority.
