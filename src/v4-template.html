    06 / The dock journal

    Know the finish.
    Keep it right.

    Straight answers on detailing, protection and keeping a boat ready for South Florida.

    Protection

    Wax or ceramic?
    Start with the boat.

    How condition, use and your maintenance routine shape the right recommendation.

    We start with the surface. A clean hull that is already in shape calls for a different approach from gelcoat that needs correction.

    Detail & Wax pairs a Full Detail with machine-applied wax. Our ceramic packages include preparation matched to the boat. We never coat over oxidation or wax.

    Tell us how the boat is stored, how often it runs and the care it gets between trips. We will explain the preparation and protection in your custom quote.

    Maintenance

    How often should
    we wash your boat?

    The schedule should follow the way you store and use the boat.

    Start with how the boat lives: in a Slip, on a lift, or in the driveway. Then tell us how often you use it and what care happens after a trip.

    Silver offers weekly, bi-weekly or monthly maintenance washes. Gold and Platinum let us build a custom cadence around your boat, with a Full Detail every month.

    The membership tier sets the scope of care and scheduling priority. Wash frequency is a separate choice. We put both in your plan.

    From the crew

    Before we quote,
    show us the finish.

    A few useful photos help us understand the boat and the work it needs.

    Send a view of the whole boat, the area that concerns you, and a closer look at the finish. Include the length, make and location.

    If the boat has a tower or multiple decks, show those too. Surface area matters, and boats 50 feet or longer are quoted individually.

    A photo helps start the conversation. We confirm the work, access and schedule with you before the job.

    08 / Good to know

    Straight
    answers.

    Do I need to be there?

    No. Give us access and we handle it. Photos land in your Owner Portal when we are done, so you can see the work wherever you are.

    How long does a Full Detail take?

    Timing depends on length, condition and the work needed. We confirm the schedule when we quote your boat.

    Wax or ceramic?

    We help you choose based on the boat's finish, use and maintenance plan. Preparation comes first: we never coat over oxidation or wax.

    How often should we schedule a wash?

    We match the cadence to how the boat is stored and used. Silver offers weekly, bi-weekly or monthly washes. Gold and Platinum offer a custom cadence.

    How do the plans help with stains and pests?

    Silver removes light staining and helps prevent it from returning through consistent care. Gold adds monthly deeper care to remove mild staining and help prevent both light and mild staining. Frequent cleaning also clears webs, residue and hiding spots, making the boat less inviting to spiders and ants. This is cleaning-based prevention rather than a pesticide treatment.

    What happens if it rains?

    We watch the forecast and move the visit rather than rush the job. You hear from us the day before, and we offer the next slot that works.

    Do you work on boats over 50 feet?

    Yes. Boats 50 ft and over, or with a tower or multiple decks, are quoted individually to account for their surface area. Send us the boat and we will discuss the scope.

    Do you offer hull cleaning?

    We can coordinate hull cleaning for in-water boats. Tell us the location and we will confirm the scope when we quote.

    What if something needs another look?

    Tell us and we come back. Platinum owners can flag a spot in their Slip. Reporting is always free; if it needs a bigger job, we quote the work before doing it.
